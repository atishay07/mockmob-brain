## 1. Phone scroll v2: a normal swipe is exactly one step, a flick is free (LK192)

Why v1 (live) feels uneven: the step is read from the page's position, a tour step is only 326 px of scroll, and an
ordinary thumb swipe travels 300-900 px with its momentum, so one swipe moves one, two or three tour slides, and a short
swipe that stops under half a step goes back ("too little"). Held (before) made every gesture one step but also every
flick, with a wall at each story's door and a 420 ms lock: "locked in". He wants: normal scrolling consistent, flicks
free, not "properly held".

The rule to build (touch screens only; a mouse keeps one gesture, one step):
1. Native scroll stays: no touch-action lock, no preventDefault, no lock time. Inside a pinned stage nothing visible
   moves under a finger anyway, so absorbing a swipe's momentum there is invisible.
2. Early answer: as soon as the finger has moved 32 px net (vertical-dominant) from a gesture that began with the page
   resting in a flowing story, show step k+1 (or k-1) at once, while the finger is still down. Dragging back within 16 px
   of the start shows k again. One finger drag shows at most one step, however far it travels.
3. At touchend read the release speed (storyGesture.mjs `velocity(samples)`, px/ms):
   - NORMAL (under 2.2 px/ms): the gesture is exactly one step. Set keepStory/keepY to the rest of k+dir so the momentum
     is put back each frame (the existing onFrame hold); do NOT call stopFling for this (a page restyle per swipe would
     jank; the per-frame put is enough, the stage is sticky). If there is no step that way (last step going down, first
     going up), set no hold: the page leaves naturally.
   - FLICK (2.2 px/ms or more): free. The steps follow the page's position as it flies (the observer), and the scroll
     settles on the nearest rest when it stops. While a flick's momentum runs, debounce the shown step by about 120 ms
     (trailing), so slides do not strobe past: the tab ink may move, the content settles where the flick ends.
4. Arriving: a NORMAL swipe (or its momentum) that brings the page into a story from above always lands on step 0, from
   below on the last step (every time, not only the first). While a finger that began outside is down inside the story,
   keep the entry step shown. A FLICK passes, except the once-per-story first landing that v1 already has (`lands(s)`).
5. The observer in ScrollStory.jsx must not pick steps while the controller governs a gesture: add
   `controller.governs(handle)` (true from the early answer until the settle of a NORMAL gesture, and while an entering
   finger is down) and skip `applyStep(i)` in the IntersectionObserver callback and in `fromSide` while it is true.
6. Longer steps for the flick case ("increasing the time of each, making it less jumpy"): FLOW_SHARE 0.6 -> 0.85 (and
   the 0.6 in scroll-story.css `--ss-run`), so a flick passes about three tour slides, not six. Re-fit the touch reserved
   heights in landing.css afterwards (tmp/1110-convert/measure.mjs prints real section heights; two window heights give
   the px + svh fit).
7. Constants in storyGesture.mjs: FLOW_COMMIT_PX = 32, FLOW_CANCEL_PX = 16, FLOW_FLICK_V = 2.2, FLOW_PASS_DEBOUNCE_MS = 120.

Where: `src/components/landing/storyController.mjs` (onTouchStart: remember `g.flow = holding()` when it flows, `g.k0`,
`g.pageY`; onTouchMove: the early answer, before the `claim === 'native'` return, never preventDefault; onTouchEnd /
afterNativeTouch(done): classify and set the hold; onFrame: the entry rule for NORMAL momentum crossing an edge; settle:
unchanged), `ScrollStory.jsx` (governs check, debounce), `storyGesture.mjs`, `scroll-story.css`.
