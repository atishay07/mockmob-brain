When the owner sets a conversion ambition for the website, treat it as a design-quality bar ("because this is so good,
how likely is an actual student to convert, hypothetically"), not a request to pull historic sign-ups, compute rates or
set up measurement. Go deep on UI/UX and taste across the whole page, make changes he can see, and deploy live on your
own judgement once satisfied.

**Why:** on 10 Nov 2026 he answered a pass that spent much of its effort on funnel evidence and shipped three small
changes: "you don't need to see historic signups ... or calculate conversion rate"; "small, meaningful changes, but I
think you can take it deeper and improvise a lot more"; "more as a UI/UX refinement and design taste refinement ...
[conversion] more as a byproduct"; "When you are satisfied ... commit the site and deploy it live" (ledger LK189).

**How to apply:** for landing work, spend the time looking at every section at 390 and 1440, Day and Night, and
refining it within [[web-restructure-rejected]] (refine, never rebuild or cut) and [[mockmob-design-direction]]; give
at most a short hypothetical read on conversion. Do not ask him to decide measurement switches. Background numbers are
in [[conversion-baseline-and-funnel-switch]] if ever needed.
