On 16 Oct 2026 (evening) the owner said: "say time in 12-hour format, and your time range is a bit different than IST... mention it usually as 'in the next 30 minutes,' 'in the next 1 hour'". And: "It's not like this timeline is to be followed by one human... the entire 5- to 6-hour timeline that you mentioned, is achievable in 2 hours."

**Why:** I gave wall-clock ranges (and they read as off from IST) and sized plans as if one person did the work, while many subagents run in parallel.

**How to apply:** give relative times ("in the next 30 minutes", "within the hour"); if a clock time is needed, 12-hour IST (e.g. 7:30 pm). Size plans for parallel agents and say what is truly serial (gates, device walks, owner steps). Never repeat an estimate without new evidence.
