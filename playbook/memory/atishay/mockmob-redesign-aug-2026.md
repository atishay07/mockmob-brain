Offered a rolled, out-of-category visual world twice (impeccable `concept-seed --scope direction --mode persuade`, seeds `c4a83c96` then `b256fb04`), the owner re-rolled the first and then deliberately took the **standing exit — the category standard, executed at full craft**. They then named the craft bar as **Brilliant / Duolingo plus the Indian EdTech leaders (Physics Wallah, Unacademy, Allen)**, and kept volt green `#D2F000` while releasing everything else.

**Why:** this owner does not want MockMob to look unlike an EdTech product. They want it to look like the *best* one — familiar structure, unfamiliar execution quality. Their "remove the AI slop" instruction was about craft and defaults, not about inventing a novel visual world.

**How to apply:** on this project, propose conventional structure executed to a high bar rather than out-of-category concepts, and put the craft budget into type, spacing, motion and honest proof. Do not skip the roll — they still want the mechanism run and the alternatives shown — but expect canon to win, and do not argue them out of it. Anything they pick becomes a brand commitment in `PRODUCT.md`. See [[mockmob-owner-audit-jul-2026]].
