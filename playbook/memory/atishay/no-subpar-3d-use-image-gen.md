**Why:** the generated Mobi stills already on the site set the bar; a hand-built model could not match them in a day, and he sees the images I open while working, so rough intermediate renders read to him as the output.

**How to apply:**

- "3D" asked of me means the look and motion of the page (depth, CSS 3D, WebGL effects that can be made right), not replacing art that already looks better.
- He watches working screenshots live: an early, rough render shown in the session is judged as if it were the result. Iterate privately on numbers and geometry where I can, and say plainly when something I am opening is a first pass (my interpretation of how to avoid a repeat).
- Stop early: when a first render is clearly below the existing art, change course at once instead of iterating in view.
