The owner rejected round 1 of the MockMob promo teaser (a calm, small-scale, dark Claude Motion web artifact with captions instead of voice) as "way off what I wanted". The target is the TixFox and Pocketsflow reference films (Downloads\mbnrH8INtduXj8GW.mp4, ZN2B5J7DqR2itqzt.mp4; also Stripe Video-83924 and Duolingo Video-56055): chapters on flat colour grounds, headline words appearing in sync with the voice-over with highlight boxes, one bold neo-brutalist UI card updating on the spoken words, a mascot avatar, whip transitions, huge type, a cut every 0.5-1.2 s, a rendered MP4. Preparoo (Video-18960) is content flow only.

**Why:** he said "this is the kind of result that is expected... keep going until you achieve this level", and that sound design (voice choice, SFX, pacing, matching voice to graphics and effects) is one of the most important parts.

**How to apply:** for any MockMob film work, build in Remotion (C:\Users\atish\Desktop\MockMob-promo-film, PRODUCTION.md is the bible), generate the voice first with open-source TTS and drive every word, card change and effect from its word timestamps; compare every render's contact sheet to the reference sheets in docs/ref before showing him. He expects us to use any available tools (open-source models, ChatGPT images) and to iterate autonomously. Related: [[mockmob-design-direction]], [[quality-bar-and-stop-rule]], [[build-and-look-loop]].

**21 Oct 11:20 pm addition (tactile layer):** he says a great motion film without adequate sound design is pointless, and that premium SaaS explainers have sound that makes you feel the UI like haptics. Every MockMob film needs a tactile layer: a crisp, dry, quiet, unpitched micro-sound in frame-exact sync under every visible UI interaction (taps, ticks, landings, slides, type-on, screen swaps), felt more than heard, never toy-like. The spec is DIRECTOR v6.3 addendum 5 in the film's docs/NOTES.md.
