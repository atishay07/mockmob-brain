On 21 Oct 2026 (about 11 am) the owner watched teaser v2 and said the whole thing feels "very childish, like it is built for kids, like a toy". The animations look good. The biggest cause is the sound: the voice (Kokoro hf_alpha, young and bright) is "very childish", and the SFX and background music make it "very weird". He wants the "serious look" of the reference films (TixFox, Pocketsflow, Stripe). The audience is 16-17-year-olds, but they should not be talked down to.

**Why:** our sound was the opposite of the references. They are voice films with a near-silent ghost-tick or bass bed and about 0.5 effects a second. We shipped a melodic General MIDI score (FluidSynth + MuseScore_General.sf2), 36 synthesised toy SFX (pops, boings, chime arpeggios) and 171 cues, 108 of them micro. "Bright and bouncy" from the question round was read too literally. The voice was also chosen by machine scores without anyone listening.

**How to apply:**
- The voice must sound mature, confident and unhurried, with no sing-song delivery. The owner picks it by ear from a short casting pack; machine scores only shortlist.
- Music should be minimal and produced (real drum and bass samples or a licensed library track), with no GM melody.
- SFX should be few, subtle and realistic (air whooshes, paper foley, muted clicks), with no toy pops or chimes.
- Keep effect density near the references (about 0.5-0.8 a second).

Later on 21 Oct the owner upgraded ElevenLabs to Starter (30k credits). He shortlisted Malar and Himanshu on eleven_v4. He also set three more rules:
- The voice must never act out on-screen events (no spoken "Submit").
- The drama of the reveal comes from SFX, animation and silence.
- The graphics should go 3D, and Blender 4.2 is installed.

The full ledger is in the film project at docs/OWNER-LEDGER.md; read it first.
