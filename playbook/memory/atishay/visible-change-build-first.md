On 13 Nov 2026 the owner looked at round 13 (a motion tune, a small Mobi on the story bar, an Ask Mobi key shown only after the five sample questions) and said he could see one change, the hopping Mobi, and "the entire landing page looks the same to me". Then: "I don't want you to think again and again ... go on to the building part ... Don't start looking again or reading stuff again ... Only review once you have completed all the criteria ... and you can score at least above an 8".

**Why:** he judges on his phone in a minute of scrolling; changes measured in milliseconds, or a feature placed behind five taps, do not exist for him. It repeats [[ui-audit-log-first]] (12 Nov: visible change, not hours of looking).

**How to apply:** when he asks for a pass, lead with changes a returning visitor sees in the first screens, and put a new feature where it is met without effort. Keep subtle fixes as a side dish and say they are subtle. Decide, build in parallel, and hold the review to one pass at the end with a score per criterion he named. My reading, not his words: "controlled" in his briefs means tasteful and uncluttered, not small.
