The owner (19 Oct 2026, 1:15 am, after testing v15.2 and release 10): MockMob is for 16-17-year-olds, many new to using mobile apps and sitting a CBT for the first time. If he finds the UI confusing or overwhelming, they certainly will. A fresh account must be complete without Pro or payment ("bare minimum, or we can't even put the app into testing").

**Why:** after many agent rounds he still found the core experience confusing, missing pieces on a new account (one suggested card, no NTA/Smart mock, the Mob hidden in You), and regressions (the result reveal, website gradients) the agents never noticed themselves.

**How to apply:** check every screen as a first-time 17-year-old on a fresh account, not only on a seeded account. Hiding a feature (e.g. behind a flag) is a regression unless he asked for it. Every walk includes a fresh-account path and a "would a new student understand this" pass. Compare against the previous release for lost polish before shipping.
