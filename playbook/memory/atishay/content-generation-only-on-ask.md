On 17 Oct 2026 about 10:25 am IST the owner said: "make this a standing instruction that generating questions or content is to be done whenever I say only, not automatically running these with every prompt."

**Why:** content wave 4 (300 questions, 186 agents, about 23 M subagent tokens) ran alongside the website and app work he was waiting for, burned a large share of the usage window, and competed for RAM while none of his 17 Oct asks were live yet. He judges progress by the website and the app.

**How to apply:**
- Do not start question authoring, scoring, publishing, bank re-validation or any other content-generation wave unless the owner asks for it in his own current words.
- A handoff, a next-session prompt, a plan or an older "keep adding depth" line does not count as asking. When writing handoffs and prompts, list content work as "only when the owner asks".
- Finishing or publishing a wave he already asked for is fine only when he has asked for it in the current session; otherwise report it as ready and wait.
- Product work on the website and the app comes first. See [[features-over-retesting]], [[agent-swarm-resource-hygiene]].
