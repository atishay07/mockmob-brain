On 19 Nov 2026 (about 4:20 pm) the owner tested the desktop site and said it is buggier and less impressive than the phone one, though "desktop is our best experience". His words: "on desktop, you can easily go way more all out than on mobile when adding even the cursor animations, animating cursors, scrolls, and clicks"; "treat mobile and desktop as separate optimizations"; scroll "stuck or very jumpy"; marks "overlapping a bit too much with the other letters"; "too much white space in between stuff".

**Why:** every pass since 10 Nov judged the phone only ([[phone-is-the-product]]); desktop was never walked with a mouse wheel, so the held stories swallowed the wheel unnoticed.

**How to apply:** after any landing change, also walk it at 1440 with a real wheel (`tmp/1119-phone/wheel.mjs`, `gaps.mjs`) and look at every mark against the neighbouring letters. Desktop may carry richer motion than phone (fine-pointer only). My reading: phone stays first for conversion, desktop must not be the weaker one.
