On 16 Nov 2026 (about 1 am IST) the owner saw my first Daily Five redesign (a thin "ticket" row: small calendar leaf, tiles, ink key) and said it was "still not up to the mark", showed Prepium's weekly-dose cards (large date panel, live dot, chips, big key) and said: do not copy them (he calls their UI and ours "AI sloppy"), "think in a unique brand identity direction", "this space is a bit low" (give the feature more room), "not always taking the safest approach, is usually the best approach", "a highly converting website is very important", and "make sure that it's very clearly depicted: what it is exactly and how we do it every time". He approved the second version ("Yes, that's what I want"): a large tear-off calendar page, the date huge on an ink panel with the live clock, volt accents, chips that state the rhythm, a full-width volt key.

**Why:** quiet rows and small refinements read to him as weak and generic; the landing must sell each feature.

**How to apply:** when presenting a feature on the landing or its own page, give it a large, confident block with one clear idea drawn from MockMob's own world (paper, ink, volt, the desk, Mobi), state what it is and when it renews in plain chips or a line, and use a full-size key. Do not default to "quiet row, never volt". Still no copying a rival's look. My reading: "one volt key per view" bends for a feature's own block on the landing.
