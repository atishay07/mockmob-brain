For the MockMob website, design, judge and verify the phone first (390 and 360 wide), laptop second. On the landing
page the lead may add or remove anything if it converts better: remove repeats, keep each feature's best demo once.

**Why:** 10 Nov 2026, after round 10 went live: "All this I have seen on the phone only because that's where 100% of
our conversions will take place ... That is our primary product." "You have the authority to add or remove anything on
the landing page. The goal is to make it the best for conversion and converting students into paying customers." The
phone looked "90% the same" to him because much of round 10's visible change was on laptops. He also found the new
flow scroll uneven in normal swipes ("skips too much or too little"), flicks running on is fine, held is not wanted.

**How to apply:** report phone changes first and say exactly where to look; spend the effort on the phone view. Scroll:
a normal swipe = exactly one step, a flick = free ([[phone-scroll-flow-model]]; spec in
`mockmob-brain/docs/ROUND-11-SPEC-2026-11-10.md`, ledger LK191-LK198). This is newer than "never cut" in
[[web-restructure-rejected]] for the landing page only. See also [[conversion-means-taste-not-metrics]].
